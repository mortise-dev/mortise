Mortise sends nothing by itself. Reporting a problem is a link that you follow.

1. Open Settings, then **General**.
2. Press **Report a problem** under Feedback. Your browser opens the
   [problem form](https://github.com/mortise-dev/mortise/issues/new?template=problem.yml) on GitHub.
3. Check **Mortise version** and **Platform**: Mortise filled them in. They are the only things the link
   carries. It carries no path, no module name and nothing else of your app.
4. Fill in **nwidart/laravel-modules version**, **What happened** and **What you expected**, and send the form.

Do not paste paths, module names, tokens or source code that you want to keep private.

To ask for a change, use the [request form](https://github.com/mortise-dev/mortise/issues/new?template=request.yml).
Mortise covers one app, at one commit, on one machine. A request about history, a comparison between
commits, several apps or a team belongs to [Mortise Cloud](https://mortisecloud.com), and gets the label
`cloud` and an answer that says why.
