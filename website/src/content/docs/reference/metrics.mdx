The **Metrics** tab is the fifth tab of the workspace (<kbd>5</kbd>).
It shows requests, response time, errors, jobs and database time per module of a running app.
The figures come from the `prometheus` endpoint of the actuator of the environment that is connected.
Metrics use the same environment, URL and credentials as the runtime box. There is no separate Prometheus address.
The steps to connect are in [How to read metrics](/mortise/how-to/read-metrics/).

## Series

Mortise reads three families of the Prometheus text format. It skips every other family.

| Family | Gives |
|---|---|
| `http_server_request_duration_seconds` | Requests: their count, the count with a 5xx status, the total time and the buckets of the histogram |
| `messaging_process_duration_seconds` | Jobs: their count |
| `db_client_operation_duration_seconds` | Database time. Kept only for a series with a `module` label |

## Placing a series in a module

Each series counts for one module, or for **Not in a module**.

| Series | Placed by |
|---|---|
| With a `module` label | The label wins. It matches a module name without regard to case. The value `none`, or a name the app does not have, counts as **Not in a module**. An empty label counts as no label |
| A request without the label | Its `http_route` through the `mappings` of the app. It matches the routes whose path equals the route and whose methods hold its method. When none holds the method, every route with that path counts |
| A job without the label | The namespace of its `laravel_job_name` |

A request counts as **Not in a module** when:

- its routes are in two modules, such as one path on two domains (the metric drops the domain);
- it has no `http_route`, such as a 404 or a 405;
- its route belongs to no module, such as a closure or a route of a package;
- the app does not expose `mappings`. The tab then says
  "Requests without a module label are not placed in a module, because mappings is not exposed on local."

Placing runs on the files and the `mappings` of now. A read kept from before a change of routes is placed by the new routes.

## Reads

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Pace | One read every 15 s |
| When | Only while the Metrics tab is drawn and the window is visible |
| Kept | In memory, for 24 hours. Nothing is written to disk |
| Thinned | In the last hour, every read is kept. Before that, one read per 5 minutes |
| Gap | A read more than three paces after the one before starts a new run. The chart draws no segment across the gap |
| Limits | An answer over 16 MB, or one that needs more than 32,768 figures or 4 MB of series names, is not read |

A rate needs two reads. Until the second read every figure is an en dash, and a line says
"A rate needs two reads. The second read comes 15 s after the first."

## Connected with data

### Top line

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Environment switch | Chooses the environment. It is the same choice as the runtime box and the index header. It shows with one environment too |
| Range switch | **15 min**, **1 hour** (the default) or **24 hours**. Changing it keeps the reads |
| Read line | "read every 15 s, last read 8 s ago" |

### Chart

The chart shows the requests per minute of the selected row. With no row selected it shows the whole scope:
"Requests per minute, all modules", or "Requests per minute, Billing" for one module.
The note at the right reads "842 now". Under the axis, the left end reads "1 hour ago" (or "15 min ago", "24 hours ago")
and the right end reads "now".
For a screen reader the chart has a summary: "Now 842, highest 910, lowest 12 requests per minute." With no point: "No rate yet."

### Table

For **All modules**, one row per module of the app, then **Not in a module**.
For one module, one row per route of the module.
Rows sort by most requests first, then by name.

| Column | Value |
|---|---|
| **Module**, or **Route** for one module | The code stamp and the name, or the method and route, such as `GET /billing/invoices/{invoice}`. A request with a `module` label and no route reads "GET without a route" |
| **Requests/min** | Over the range. One decimal under 10, such as "4.0", else a whole number |
| **95% under** | The smallest bucket bound under which 95% of the requests fall, such as "100 ms", or "over 10 s" past the largest bound. It is a bound of the histogram, not an exact time |
| **Errors** | The share of requests answered with a 5xx status, such as "2.4%" |
| **Jobs/min** | Over the range. An en dash for a route |
| **Database time** | Database time against request time, such as "31%". **unknown**, with the tooltip "Needs a newer nwidart/laravel-modules", when no series has a `module` label. An en dash for a route |
| **Last hour** | A sparkline of the requests per minute over the last hour |

A click on a row shows it in the chart. <kbd>J</kbd>, <kbd>K</kbd> and the arrows move the selected row while the table has focus.
The colour of the figures is ink only: this version has no limits, so nothing on the tab is a state.

## States

| State | Title | Text | Controls |
|---|---|---|---|
| No environment | **Metrics are not connected** | "Metrics come from the actuator of the running app. Connect an environment for shop-api to read requests, response time and errors per module." | **Connect an environment** |
| Before the first read | "Reading metrics from local" | none | none |
| `prometheus` not exposed | "Metrics are not exposed on local" | "Add prometheus to exposure.include in config/actuator.php, next to health." | **Try again**, **Open settings** |
| A 200 with no text | "Metrics are off on local" | "The app answers with no metrics. Set METRICS_ENABLED=true in its .env to turn them on." | **Try again** |
| A 503 | "Metrics cannot be read on local" | "local answered 503 for /actuator/prometheus: "…" Check the metrics store of the app, then try again." | **Try again** |
| Text with no `# TYPE` line | "The answer of local is not metrics" | "local answered prometheus with text that holds no metrics. Check that the address points to the actuator of the app." | **Try again**, **Open settings** |
| Over 16 MB | "The metrics of local are too large" | "The answer is larger than 16 MB, so Mortise does not read it. Export fewer series from the app." | **Try again** |
| Too many series | "The metrics of local have too many series" | "Mortise keeps at most 32,768 figures per read. Export fewer series from the app." | **Try again** |
| No answer, refused credentials, another status | The word of the index header, such as "No answer on local" | The sentence of the runtime box | **Try again**, **Open settings** |
